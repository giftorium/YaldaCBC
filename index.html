The Night When Time Stands Still

Yalda is Iran’s winter solstice vigil—fruit, flame, and verses keeping the night awake until first light slips back.

What is Yalda?

The longest night of the year is called Yalda—“birth”—because we sit with the dark and trust dawn to return if we wait together.

Fruit on the Table

Pomegranates and watermelon keep a slice of summer glowing on the table. Each ruby seed swears winter’s chill won’t win.

Hafez Speaks

A Fāl-e Hafez starts with a wish and a random page. The poet answers in layered lines that feel like a friend in candlelight.

Hold the Light

Candles, dried fruit, elders, and laughter keep the room awake. The night softens when we share it shoulder to shoulder.
